Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CustomGiftLab

How long does it take to produce my custom item?

Most custom items take 3-5 business days to produce. After production, shipping time depends on your location and selected shipping method.

What file formats are accepted for custom designs?

We accept PNG, JPG, and SVG files. For best results, use high-resolution images (at least 300 DPI) with transparent backgrounds when needed.

Are your products eco-friendly?

Yes, we are committed to sustainability. Our products are made with eco-friendly materials and processes, and our packaging is recyclable.

Can I return a custom product?

Due to the personalized nature of our products, custom items cannot be returned unless there is a manufacturing defect. Please review your design carefully before ordering.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 100 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination.

How durable are your custom prints?

Our prints are made with high-quality inks and printing techniques that ensure durability. They are resistant to fading and peeling with proper care.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

You can cancel your order within 24 hours of purchase. Once production has begun, cancellation is no longer possible.

What sizes are available for your products?

We offer various sizes for different products. Specific size options are available on each product page. If you need a custom size, please contact us.

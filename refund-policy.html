Refund Policy

Information about refunds and returns for CustomGiftLab products

Last Updated: July 6, 2023

Due to the customized nature of our products, our refund policy differs from standard retail policies. Please read carefully before placing your order.

Orders in Production

Once an order enters the production phase, it cannot be canceled or refunded. Custom items are made specifically for you and cannot be resold. We recommend reviewing your design carefully before submitting your order.

Manufacturing Defects

If you receive a product with a manufacturing defect (e.g., printing errors, material flaws), please contact us within 30 days of receipt. We will work with you to resolve the issue, which may include a replacement or refund.

Incorrect Orders

If you provided incorrect information during the ordering process (e.g., wrong size, spelling errors), we cannot offer a refund. However, we may offer a discount on a replacement order at our discretion.

Damage During Shipping

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of receipt. We will investigate the claim and provide a resolution, which may include a replacement or refund.

Processing Time

Refunds, when applicable, are processed within 5-10 business days after approval. The refund will be issued to the original payment method.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our refund policy, please contact us at support@customgiftlab.com or use our contact form.

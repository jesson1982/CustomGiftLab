Photo Gift Design Tips

By Lauren Fisher | Updated: 2026-08-12

Short answer: great photo gifts start with the photo: pick high-resolution, well-lit images with clear faces, then use a simple layout with one hero photo and one to three supporting shots.

Bottom line: check the design preview at full zoom before approving, because most photo gift disappointments come from low-resolution sources that looked fine at thumbnail size.

Turn your favorite memories into beautiful custom gifts

Introduction to Photo Gifts

Photo gifts are among the most personal and meaningful custom items you can create. Whether it is a family portrait on a mug, a vacation snapshot on a blanket, or a candid moment on a pillow, photo gifts preserve memories in a tangible, everyday way.

Selecting the Right Photos

Resolution and Quality

Always use the highest resolution photo available. Photos should be at least 300 DPI at the intended print size. Smartphone photos from modern cameras are usually sufficient, but avoid heavily cropped or zoomed images.

Lighting and Composition

Well-lit photos print better. Avoid dark, underexposed images. Photos with good contrast and clear subjects work best. Consider the background and ensure the main subject is clearly visible.

Photo Editing Tips

  • Adjust brightness and contrast for better print results
  • Crop to focus on the main subject
  • Remove red-eye and minor blemishes
  • Apply subtle sharpening if the image appears soft
  • Avoid heavy filters that may look different when printed

Design Layout Strategies

Single Photo Design

One powerful photo can make a strong impact. Center the image and leave adequate margins. This works especially well for mugs, canvas prints, and single-image blankets.

Collage Design

Combine multiple photos into a cohesive layout. Use consistent spacing, choose a unifying color scheme, and vary photo sizes for visual interest. Collages work beautifully on blankets and pillows.

Photo with Text

Combine photos with meaningful text like dates, names, or quotes. Ensure text is readable against the background. Use contrasting colors for text overlays.

File Preparation

  • Save as high-quality PNG or JPEG
  • Use sRGB color space for accurate color reproduction
  • Set resolution to at least 300 DPI at print size
  • Include bleed area if the design extends to edges
  • Remove any watermarks or copyrighted material

Product-Specific Tips

Photo Mugs

Use horizontal or square photos. Avoid placing important elements near the handle. Wraparound designs work well for panoramic photos.

Photo Blankets

Collages with 6-20 photos work beautifully. Leave space between photos for the fleece texture to show through. Use high-contrast images for best results.

Photo Pillows

Center the main subject. Remember that pillow edges will curve, so keep important elements away from borders. Square photos work best for square pillows.

Conclusion

Creating stunning photo gifts is easier than ever with these tips. By selecting quality photos, designing thoughtful layouts, and preparing files correctly, you can create personalized gifts that preserve your most cherished memories. Browse our photo gift collection to get started.

Frequently Asked Questions
What resolution do photos need for custom photo gifts?

Aim for 300 DPI at print size. Phone photos taken in good light usually work for pillows and mugs; large blankets need the highest resolution source you have.

How many photos work best on a collage gift?

One hero photo plus two to four supporting photos reads cleanest. Beyond six photos, individual images shrink enough that faces lose impact at viewing distance.
